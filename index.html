About Peter

Experience you can use—not theory you have to translate.

Peter Stemmet is an international broadcaster, presenter and moderator with more than two decades of experience across television, radio and live events.

As a presenter with Al Jazeera English, he has covered major global sporting events including the FIFA World Cup, Olympic Games, Asian Cup and Africa Cup of Nations. That experience has taught him what makes communication work when there is no second take.

His style is calm, direct and practical. The goal is not to make people sound rehearsed. It is to help them sound clear, credible and fully themselves.
